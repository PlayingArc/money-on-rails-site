What Money on Rails is

Money on Rails is a read-only analytics companion for your YNAB plan. It connects to YNAB through YNAB’s own API, with your authorization, to compute and display an Adherence Rating, a Plan Quality Rating, and a category-expense breakdown that YNAB doesn’t natively show you. We never write to, modify, or delete anything in your actual YNAB plan.
